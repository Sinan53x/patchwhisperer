# Meta state
Last patch: Minor Update - 11-04-2026 (2026-11-05)

## Thesis
Games are still won by converting a lead into Guardians/Walkers before the comeback economy catches up — the 10-30 economy, farm routes and objective clock are untouched. What moved is the answer to sustain: Healbane's multi-source anti-heal now caps at 72% instead of 82%, so stacking anti-heal across a team is inefficient and one dedicated buyer is correct. Heal-and-grind frontlines hold ground they previously lost, and burst-pick comps lose their anti-heal coverage slot.

## Economy and systems
- Comeback: non-hero comeback gold capped at 30%, tick souls routed to the two lowest-econ players, deficit-scaled secure rate up to 50% faster on contested Haunt souls. Sitting on a lead is a losing line.
- Kill income stays contested: the Soul Bag must be last-hit after the kill.
- Bounties: Guardian +10%, Walker +5%, objective share 30->25%.
- Objective parries no longer reset the parry cooldown — Guardian/Shrine dives are one-shot.
- Respawn 38s at 20m. Global slows -20%; air drag ratio 0.5 — airborne exits slower, ground kiting stronger, flyers taxed.
- Steam Vents grant +150% stamina regen, no invisibility. Cast-on-press holds; Healing Snacks 10% max HP.
- Anti-heal: Healbane single source unchanged; 2 sources 54.7%, 4 sources 72% (was 57.8%/82.2%). One buyer per team is the correct doctrine.

## Map
Lanes renamed; four districts plus off-lane corridors. All neutrals are one of nine Haunt types that group and chase — bounty and growth +10%, HP and damage +10%. Tough Crates (58 souls, Heavy Melee only), Barrel Mimics, Sinner's Sacrifice variants, and Buff Containers rolling permanent Move Speed, Bullet/Spirit Resist and ability range; a Halloween candy-cauldron layer rewards Heavy Melee. Hubs: Sunken Plaza (~5-8 min, stamina strip) and Bell Tower (8+, ~280 souls, one rope, three vaults, map-wide bell). Green lane holds a roof/tree above the bridge. Arch Mother's side owns Sunken Plaza plus the theater.

## Archetype standing
- Dedicated healers: up — the stacked anti-heal cap cut is a direct reprieve (Kelvin, Ivy, Rem, Mo & Krill).
- Anti-heal coverage buyers: down — Healbane as a team slot is worth less; single-source value is flat (Seven, Infernus, Yamato, Drifter).
- In-fight lifesteal bruisers: up — softer anti-heal on top of the 10-30 lifesteal cluster (Victor, Billy, Shiv, Abrams).
- AoE one-cycle camp clearers: stable — farm engine untouched (Seven, Infernus, McGinnis, Mina, Yamato).
- Heavy-melee frontline/tanks: up slightly — Mo & Krill's Scorn/Combo buffs; Baba's bruiser shell thinner on every axis.
- Out-of-combat regen sustain: down — Radiant Regeneration is combat-only, Healing Booster delinked and trimmed (Baba, Shiv, Lady Geist, Celeste).
- Max-range poke/gun carries: down — HVR velocity 60->45%, Express Shot -5%, worse air drag (Vindicta, Grey Talon, Paige).
- Roamers/pick assassins: mixed — no free vent approach, Shadow Weave needs 6s setup, but stamina vents feed re-engages (Lash, Holliday, Paradox, Calico).
- Lane bullies: up — Apollo's Riposte now disarms and strips parries; the comeback floor still blunts snowball conversion.
- Late scalers: up — non-hero comeback gold and tick souls fund the slow curve (Pocket, Mirage, Warden).
- Flyers: down — air drag 0.5 plus reliable grounding; Deadman Danny trimmed again (Grey Talon, Celeste, Vindicta, Deadman Danny).

## Watchlist
- Healbane buys per team per game — if the double-buy rate does not fall, the patch's only meta lever did nothing.
- Kelvin, Ivy and Rem win rates — the cleanest read on whether the anti-heal cap restored healer value.
- Mo & Krill pick rate against win rate — largest single-hero gain; expect a pick-rate bump before a win-rate one.
- Celeste pick rate — a C->D full-kit trim should show as near-total disappearance, not a dip.
- Average game length — expected flat; if it rises, sustain comps are stalling out the comeback clock.
- Graves snapshot 56.5% WR / 40.9% PR still conflicts with the KB's 48.5% / 21% — recheck.
