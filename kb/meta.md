# Meta state
Last patch: Minor Update - 11-04-2026 (2026-11-05)

## Thesis
Games are still won by converting a lead into Guardians and Walkers before the comeback economy pays the trailing team, and off-lane Haunt farm is still the income engine for both sides. What changed is the mid-fight: stacked anti-heal is weaker, so kit healers and self-sustain bruisers hold their bars longer, and the outlier frontlines (Baba, Celeste, Deadman Danny) were trimmed. One-cycle camp clearers who spend a lead early and late scalers with a farm floor remain the winners — the fight just lasts longer now.

## Economy and systems
- Comeback: non-hero comeback gold capped at 30% (was 26%), tick souls routed to the two lowest-econ players, deficit-scaled secure rate up to 50% faster on contested Haunt souls. Sitting on a lead is a losing line.
- Kill income stays contested: the Soul Bag must be last-hit after the kill. Bounties: Guardian +10%, Walker +5%, objective share 30->25% (09-16, holds).
- Objective parries no longer reset the parry cooldown — Guardian/Shrine dives are one-shot.
- Respawn 38s at 20m. Global slows -20%; air drag ratio 0.5 — airborne exits slower, ground kiting stronger, flyers taxed.
- Steam Vents keep invisibility and add +150% stamina regen. Cast-on-press holds; Healing Snacks 10% max HP.
- Anti-heal: Healbane's first source is unchanged, but stacking is trimmed (2 sources 57.8->54.7%, 4 sources 82.2->72%, stacks decay 20% faster). Anti-heal is a slot again, not a team coverage plan.

## Map
Lanes renamed; four districts plus off-lane corridors. All neutrals are one of nine Haunt types that group and chase — bounty and growth +10%, HP and damage +10%. Tough Crates (58 souls, Heavy Melee only), Barrel Mimics, Sinner's Sacrifice variants, and Buff Containers rolling permanent Move Speed, Bullet/Spirit Resist and ability range; a Halloween candy-cauldron layer rewards Heavy Melee. Hubs: Sunken Plaza (~5-8 min, stamina strip) and Bell Tower (8+, ~280 souls, one rope, three vaults, map-wide bell). Green lane holds a roof/tree above the bridge. Arch Mother's side owns Sunken Plaza plus the theater.

## Archetype standing
- AoE one-cycle camp clearers: up — richer Haunts barely cost them HP (Seven, Infernus, McGinnis, Mina, Yamato).
- In-fight lifesteal bruisers: up — lifesteal cluster buffed while out-of-combat regen was cut (Victor, Infernus, Drifter).
- Dedicated healers: up, reversing 10-30 — the stacking system that blanked them is capped, so Kelvin, Ivy, Rem, Paige and Nurse Harrow all hold longer.
- Heavy-melee frontline/tanks: stable — Baba's resist scaling 0.7->0.5 and melee -10%/boon trim the wall, but Mo & Krill's Scorn/Burrow/Combo buffs replace it (Abrams, Mo & Krill, Billy, Shiv).
- Out-of-combat regen sustain: still down but relieved — Radiant Regeneration is combat-only, yet stacked anti-heal no longer fully suppresses it (Baba, Shiv, Lady Geist, Violet).
- Max-range poke/gun carries: down — HVR velocity, Express Shot and air drag still bite; Celeste lost 5m of cast range and most of her silence (Vindicta, Grey Talon, Paige).
- Roamers/pick assassins: mixed — slower tunnels and Shadow Weave setup, but Apollo's Riposte now disarms gun carries and Deadman Danny's execute fell to +55% (Lash, Holliday, Paradox, Calico, Apollo).
- Lane bullies: slightly down — the comeback floor blunts snowball conversion (Paige, Apollo).
- Late scalers: up — comeback gold and tick souls fund the slow curve (Pocket, Mirage, Warden).
- Flyers: down — air drag 0.5 plus reliable grounding; Deadman Danny's Salvo scaling and jetpack heal both cut (Grey Talon, Celeste, Vindicta, Deadman Danny).

## Watchlist
- Healbane purchases per team: if the average falls toward one, the coverage plan is dead and kit healers rebound further.
- Kelvin, Ivy and Rem win rates — the cleanest read on whether the stacking cap is worth ~3 points of heal throughput.
- Deadman Danny pick and win rate: a fifth off the execute should show fast on a dive hero.
- Celeste's T3 talent pick rate: if players abandon the silence talent, her identity shifted, not just her numbers.
- Mo & Krill pick rate into frontline-heavy lobbies, as the answer to Baba and Viscous.
- Graves snapshot 57.5% WR still conflicts with the KB's 48.5% / 21% — nothing here touches her kit, so recheck.
