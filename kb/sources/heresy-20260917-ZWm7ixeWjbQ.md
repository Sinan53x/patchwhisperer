# Explaining Deadlock's Most Significant Balance Patch In A Year — HERESY (20260917)

## Meta thesis
This is a tempo meta patch: Valve gutted comeback souls and the Unstable Rift's early-game generosity, so winning lane and snowballing now actually converts into winning the game. Scaling carries (Wraith, Celeste, gun Haze builds, Warden builds) lose their safety net, while early-game tempo/snowball heroes (Paige, Apollo, Vindicta, Yamato, Silver, Holiday) rise. The biggest movers are system changes — economy, rift, parry, reload, slows, melee cycle time — not the hero number tweaks.

## Hero claims
- **Apollo** tier=None dir=rising: No direct changes, but he is one of the best early-game snowballers (ganks, wins lane, kills jungle instantly, escapes death, high flat damage/healing) and tempo now matters. The creator calls him 'super buffed this patch' despite being absent from the notes.
- **Abrams** tier=None dir=rising: Only tweaked, same character, but the dash/light-melee no longer interrupting gun cycle time lets him weave dashes and punches between shots for a more fluid lane.
- **Celeste** tier=None dir=falling: Nerfed seven different ways with no buffs, but the bigger hits are indirect: egg nerf, her item nerfs, scaling nerfs punishing early-game losers, and slows now affecting air drag hurting her flying.
- **Graves** tier=None dir=stable: Described as the same character; no meaningful opinion beyond that.
- **Haze** tier=None dir=rising: Gun Haze is better now with an appreciable damage increase since her damage comes from Fixation stacks, so headshots matter more; viability unclear but promising.
- **Holliday** tier=None dir=rising: Being made more of a gun hybrid and lasso buffed; with tempo up and post-20-minute picks more valuable (respawn timer change), she should re-emerge with hybrid gun-spirit builds.
- **Ivy** tier=None dir=falling: Slight nerfs, which the creator approves of because the character is overpowered.
- **Kelvin** tier=None dir=rising: Frozen Shelter now has innate spirit power, so it may be usable late game without maxing it; creator wants to test builds that don't max the ability.
- **Lady Geist** tier=None dir=rising: Slight direct buffs, but mostly rises because the characters she competes with were nerfed.
- **Lash** tier=None dir=rising: Gun Lash killed in favor of stomp Lash, which the creator calls great and healthy — ground-strike Lash is back.
- **Paige** tier=None dir=rising: Triple-dip buffed: direct buffs, tempo being more important (she is a strong tempo character), and flying/flyers being nerfed so fewer Celeste/Vindicta-style threats. Creator calls her one of the biggest winners and a monster.
- **Paradox** tier=None dir=stable: Slight nerf to carbine quick-scoping annoyance; same character more or less.
- **Rem** tier=None dir=stable: Bug fixes and breakable-spawn timing shifted later; creator says this is not the enormous nerf people think (more a Calico hit).
- **Shiv** tier=None dir=rising: Knives no longer ricochet but do more current-HP damage, giving him more anti-tank killing ability — a healthy change; alt-fire now actually damages people again after past over-nerf.
- **Silver** tier=None dir=rising: Weighted Bola acting as Phantom Strike is massive; cycle-time change improves lane and mid-game fights, plus better scaling and sprint speed. Creator expects her to merge into the meta alongside Yamato.
- **Vindicta** tier=None dir=rising: Ultimate now works with ricochet and split shot simultaneously — a buff — but the main buff is tempo mattering and super-scaling being nerfed, since she is a high-tempo early gun carry.
- **Phinn** tier=None dir=falling: Nerfed across the board; every ability did too much damage. Still picked for the same identity reasons but no longer randomly tops damage while building half support items.
- **Vyper** tier=None dir=falling: Slight nerf, same character.
- **Warden** tier=None dir=falling: Nerfed across the board (bullet damage per boon, flask range/speed, Willpower debuff resistance), amplified by item nerfs and carry/scaling nerfs. Not gutted — silence cage keeps him viable — but should settle to a balanced win rate rather than 55%.
- **Wraith** tier=None dir=falling: Nerfed as a scaler (healing, armor shred, slow) and her items dipped, plus Plated Armor becoming a great anti-Wraith item; less oppressive mainly from tempo buffs and scaling nerfs, not a gutting.
- **Yamato** tier=None dir=rising: Buffed again and benefits from the tempo change; creator expects a huge uptick in play as players start practicing her again.

## Reasoning patterns
- Reads a system change's effect through win-conversion: the early Unstable Rift comeback nerf means a lane-winning team can now contest the rift, which both denies comeback souls and turns early leads into wins — so 'tempo' is buffed indirectly.
- Frames an economy redistribution as a player-behavior fix: routing excess comeback souls to the two lowest-econ players is read as killing the AFK-farming carry who out-farmed the winning team, an explicitly high-ELO problem.
- Argues a hero absent from the notes is the biggest winner: Apollo has no patch lines, but tempo mattering makes his early-game kit 'super buffed' — absence from notes is not absence of change.
- Separates direct vs indirect nerfs: Celeste and Wraith are 'less nerfed by their own lines than by' scaling nerfs, item nerfs, and slows now affecting air drag.
- Reads an item's hidden synergy: the air-drag slow change turns Life Strike's big slow into real CC, and Plated Armor's bug fix turns it into an anti-mixed-damage counter item.
- Labels a double-dip nerf: Golden Goose Egg is hit by the tempo shift and by direct nerfs, and counting stored souls toward net worth makes it unbuyable.
