# Vegas Deadlock Hero Tier List (June 2026) — Vegas (20260606)

## Meta thesis
The June patch is defined by the reworked urn (Unstable Rift): it is no longer an AoE-slug-fest where you stand on a small pad and get one-shot, it's a brawling mode with longer, in-the-air team fights and the urn moved to the top of the bridge. That shifts value away from AoE point-holders (McGinnis ult) toward sustained brawlers and utility hybrids, and Vegas says the overall game is '10 times more balanced' than the prior 6 months. The top of the meta is gun-carry-plus-utility hybrids (The Doorman, Grey Talon, Seven, Pocket, Silver, Shiv, Venator), while low-HP or coordination-dependent heroes (Paige, Apollo, Vindicta) are liabilities, especially outside high elo.

## Hero claims
- **Abrams** tier=A dir=stable: Consistently good, sees 3rd-5th pick value in competitive, strong for the team without being oppressive or one-shotting.
- **Apollo** tier=C dir=falling: 42% win rate, worst in game; the reposition rework 'feels unplayable' and carried the old kit. Vegas argues the kit needed a number tuning, not a rework.
- **Bebop** tier=C dir=falling: Just okay, no reason to pick over Paradox; the new urn also makes him worse.
- **Billy** tier=B dir=rising: Extremely strong brawler: huge HP and damage so utility is a bonus; his ult pulling people off the new urn point could be very good.
- **Calico** tier=C dir=falling: Rework feels bad; round-locked burst hero with too little damage and HP against everyone having 10 million HP and flying. 'Food.'
- **Celeste** tier=B dir=stable: Vegas thinks she's a lot better than people say and should be meta, but nobody finds consistent value yet.
- **The Doorman** tier=S dir=stable: Argues second-best hero in the game; the 59% win rate is dragged down because most players use the wrong (Vortex/web/cheat-death/kidnap) build instead of the position-one bells carry that does 50-70k damage. His door is one of the only real answers to Silver.
- **Drifter** tier=B dir=stable: One of the most balanced heroes in the game: decent early/mid/late, no hard CC beyond ult, scales off stacks and skill.
- **Dynamo** tier=B dir=stable: Balanced 'all right' hero; punishes bad positioning and provides great utility but is not a star and no longer dominates early/mid with damage.
- **Graves** tier=B dir=falling: Was top of A before the nerfs, now B; a 'victim' hero with low HP, no movement and nerfed damage who gets kidnapped off cooldown if a lane feeds. Not OP.
- **Grey Talon** tier=A dir=rising: Broken and needs nerfs. Early gun items make his early game dominant, then he builds spirit on top for free; the new Sharpshooter pushes him over the top, and his 2 has no downtime so he snowballs off three autos and a bird execute.
- **Haze** tier=C dir=falling: Barely playable; identity is now curse-out-of-invisibility into a no-aim one-shot ult. Only functions from a winning/neutral game state with map control and spare gold, otherwise she's dog.
- **Holliday** tier=B dir=stable: Fine and skill-expressive; good in high elo where pilots exist, weaker in low elo because players can't handle her movement.
- **Infernus** tier=B dir=stable: Balanced, roughly Drifter-tier; never giga-OP because he gets fully outclassed by the meta picks (Silver, Grey Talon, Doorman, Paradox) but pops off in mid elo against fairer comps.
- **Ivy** tier=B dir=stable: Very balanced overall, but Stone Form does too many things (unhexable i-frame, heal, damage, stun) and she has 4 stamina; Vegas would make it 3 stam and buff her 1.
- **Kelvin** tier=B dir=falling: Balanced, not that good, and Dome is giga-nerfed after the last patch.
- **Lady Geist** tier=A dir=rising: The new urn favors longer team fights over AoE slop, so he expects to see Geist again; he personally hates Geist meta (6000 HP gun hero spamming fire rate).
- **Lash** tier=A dir=stable: Always good, never below A; the urn moving to the top of the bridge is very big for Lash.
- **McGinnis** tier=B dir=stable: Good but no longer OP after the ult nerf; still worth taking over Drifter/Geist/Infernus/Dynamo/Graves. With AoE-on-point dead, he wants max 2/3 early, walls zoning the new urn, and big AoE heal in extended fights.
- **Mina** tier=B dir=falling: Nerfed and abandoned by 'meta tourists,' but Vegas insists she's still a good hero and the 'weak' talk is propaganda.
- **Mirage** tier=A dir=stable: Still good and surprisingly un-nerfed; his buffed 2 makes him a top-three most annoying hero (double teleport split-push, tornado spam, bullet evasion) but Vegas dislikes the direction.
- **Mo & Krill** tier=B dir=stable: Humble, good, genuinely balanced; notes Warden now has more HP and does 4x the damage.
- **Paige** tier=C dir=falling: Garbage and needs help: the only hero with ~2,000 HP against everyone at 4,800, so she's a liability that gets fully one-shot and even organized pro teams refuse to pick her.
- **Paradox** tier=A dir=falling: Was S-tier/best-in-game until the urn changes; now down a notch to A but still broken-ish.

Opening ~7,000 gold on all gun items lets him one-shot camps and towers, snowball the jungle, then cloak has zero downtime and buffed AP timing gives max Affliction faster. Huge high-elo/low-elo gap.
- **Rem** tier=A dir=rising: The new Rem healing is overpowered; a good Rem with Echo Shard and a frontline (Billy/Abrams/Warden/Shiv) full-heals a dived ally from zero to ~5,000 instantly. 'More broken than pre-patch Magnus heal.'
- **Seven** tier=A dir=stable: Broken and ignored by the community's 'low-elo stomper' excuse: 59.1% win rate at the highest elo and the best solo-queue carry in the game; better than peak Graves. Placed just below Abrams.
- **Shiv** tier=A dir=stable: Basically Silver two years ago / release-Shiv-tier: never weak, top HP, elite movement, and deals carry-level damage while buying zero damage items (green items, Cold Front).
- **Silver** tier=S dir=stable: The most broken hero in the game: first pick/first ban in competitive for 4 months, 55% win rate and highest pick rate in E6, one-shots anyone down 10k gold, 40s-cooldown ult that snowballs kills, and built-in slow/disarm/heal/spell-slinger. Never gets hotfix nerfed.
- **Sinclair** tier=B dir=stable: Just all right; niche and fun, could be OP once draft exists but pointless to sweat into versus Silver/Grey Talon.
- **Venator** tier=A dir=falling: Strongest hero that doesn't need gutting; too much HP now (had none months ago) and does a lot of damage for no skill. Vegas puts it in 'needs nerfs' and expects it reined in.
- **Victor** tier=A dir=falling: Hella good but getting nerfed with the new urn; may now be slightly worse than Billy.
- **Vindicta** tier=C dir=falling: Trash/barely playable: most counterable power-crept hero, a total victim, and still a low win rate even though every cheater plays her.
- **Viscous** tier=B dir=stable: Ambivalent: the cube is OP and Viscous is technically 'an accessory to murder' as an enabler (Esha into rescue beam reset), but playing him is a miserable loop. Low confidence on the tier.
- **Vyper** tier=B dir=rising: Actually hella good, top of 'all right'; does a lot of damage and the bullet is great utility for Mid Boss fights.
- **Warden** tier=A dir=stable: Good and people are just bad at him; ~7,000 HP, OP cage, low-skill walk-up one-shots. More HP and 4x the damage of Mo & Krill.
- **Wraith** tier=B dir=stable: Balanced slightly-above-average M1 carry with clear up/down times and actual counterplay, unlike other M1s.
- **Yamato** tier=None dir=None: Vegas starts discussing Yamato's many changes but the transcript cuts off before any verdict; no usable read.

## Reasoning patterns
- Reads a global system change into per-hero consequences: the urn rework is 'a general mode change,' but he immediately translates it to 'AoE point-holders like McGinnis's ult are way down, sustained brawlers are up.'
- Separates champion strength from build choices by win rate: Doorman's 59% is 'only not 60% because half the community plays the wrong build' (bells carry vs Vortex/kidnap), same logic he applies to old Bomb-Bebop vs Gun-Bebop.
- Splits high-elo from low-elo value and refuses to average them blindly: Graves, Holliday, Pocket and Mina are 'good in high elo, dog in low,' so tiers are stated with an elo caveat.
- Explains a hero's power through item economy rather than kit numbers: Grey Talon/Doorman dominate because the same cheap early gun items (Mystic Shot/Opening Rounds) snowball a lead you then layer spirit onto for free.
- Calls out hotfix inconsistency as a meta signal: Paige is left unplayable for months while Venator is hotfix-buffed and has his HP doubled, implying dev prioritization distorts which heroes are viable.
