# Vegas Deadlock Hero Tier List (April 2026) — Vegas (20260409)

## Meta thesis
There is a large gap between ~8-9 dominant heroes (Mirage, Lady Geist, Venator, Apollo, Mina, Celeste, Haze, Seven, Dynamo, Silver) and everyone else, which he reads as power creep: those heroes do too much with no downside while the rest are 'strong in their own right' but outshined. Late-game gun/scaling carries are the most broken category because average game length is ~10 minutes longer than a year ago, comeback gold and long death timers let losing carries recover, and spirit heroes have no late-game item equivalent to Silencer/Crippling Headshot/Inhibitor. Fights are decided by inflated HP pools (~6,000 HP, double health bars, 50/50 res, hybrid damage) where Toxic Bullets is often the only kill vector, so laning phase barely matters. He argues ~80% of the cast belongs in A/B and anything above A is 'cancer'.

## Hero claims
- **Abrams** tier=A dir=stable: Never bad at any point even from behind: tanky, has a stun, and a strong 'one build'. Echo Shard on charge lets him perma-CC while healing.
- **Apollo** tier=S dir=rising: Meta-defining spirit hero, insanely strong early but falls off, so top of S not S+. Overtuned and arguably hotfix-worthy; he wants the parry-dash distance cut and parry limited to spells, not bullets.
- **Bebop** tier=B dir=stable: Should not be C: gun build sits ~48% WR at all ranks, hook has real value, and it is skill-dependent/counterable rather than bad.
- **Billy** tier=A dir=stable: Roughly Abrams-tier, consistently strong but balanced for once. The 'must melee' idea is a noob trap; his ult as an AoE slowing hex helps into Mina/Apollo.
- **Calico** tier=A dir=stable: Decent, bottom of A at best; playable but not OP and not comparable to Apollo in the same role.
- **Celeste** tier=S dir=falling: Meta-defining spirit hero with elite mobility and strength all game, but 'not S+ anymore' — overtuned rather than broken.
- **The Doorman** tier=None dir=None: Placed in a 'cringe' category: insanely cringe but only situationally strong (kidnap/under-tower build); low-elo players overrate him. He would be good with a draft system.
- **Drifter** tier=A dir=falling: Top A, the strongest hero you can get without CC, but lacks mobility and CC so never S+. He is a Toxic Bullets bot: if the item is nerfed without compensation he becomes bad.
- **Dynamo** tier=S dir=rising: His 2 is the most OP spell in the game: an ~8s-cooldown AoE debuff remover/dispel that also refunds a charge and stamina. In a Toxic Bullets/DOT meta he AOE-cleanses the whole team, and Black Hole is one of few tools that can close out games since the base rework.
- **Graves** tier=B dir=stable: Decent/solid, not strong. Noob-bait: wall-bouncing zombies or a single Ricochet trivializes her, and she no longer carries regardless of gold.
- **Grey Talon** tier=B dir=stable: Completely fine, not bad — he rejects the 'GT is bad' take.
- **Haze** tier=S dir=rising: Hotfix-worthy broken, but only because of one max-rank ult point granting one additional ult target. Outside that she is the game's truest glass-cannon carry — squishy, no cheap escape, real risk. Best solo-climb pick in low elo with Ricochet.
- **Holliday** tier=B dir=falling: Worst hero of the patch: nerfed lane (1 regen, low base HP/damage) into a meta of tempo spirit heroes, falls off at ~16 min yet has among the worst first 6 minutes. Wants 2 regen in lane and a late game barrel scaling buff (~1.6 to 2).
- **Infernus** tier=B dir=stable: Balanced, top of decent: counterable, sub-par base HP scaling means he is burstable and can be shut down even when ahead. Newer carries with 6,000 HP and clearer counter-building are the problem, not him.
- **Ivy** tier=A dir=rising: Definition of decent, ~52% WR at all ranks, but he calls her underrated: Stone Form is the most generous I-frame window in the game and is not slow-hexable (no counterplay), she has 4 stamina, and the base ult duration is a 21s outlier that used to be ~12.
- **Kelvin** tier=A dir=stable: Good but not OP despite low-elo complaints: his ult is awful until max level and he dies through the dome early. Would be OP if nobody bought anti-heal, but he is 50-70% anti-healed in practice.
- **Lady Geist** tier=S dir=rising: Thanos-level broken and uncounterable: top-two scaler with OP lane and base HP/gun damage, the Soul Exchange plus silence-for-25m ult grants spirit, fire rate and spirit armor with no perk investment. Builds 'slop' and is OP anyway.
- **Lash** tier=None dir=stable: Like Pocket, would be broken without Curse/Counter Spell existing; he does not elaborate.
- **McGinnis** tier=None dir=None: Placed in the 'cringe but situationally strong' category with The Doorman: sometimes F-tier, sometimes S-tier; turret stacking at Mid Boss can be near-uncounterable depending on draft.
- **Mina** tier=S dir=stable: Always been OP and meta-defining; a DLC hero that does too much damage and is too movementy/uncounterable.
- **Mirage** tier=S dir=rising: Most broken/out-of-touch hero of the patch and near hotfix: max Scarabs plus a 30s-CD ult that lines up with Divine Kevlar gives ~100% uptime on a cross-map TP with ~1,500 shield, 35 spirit, 20 fire rate and 3 move speed. 'Three days away from a nuclear weapon.'
- **Mo & Krill** tier=A dir=stable: Bottom of A; solid kit, low skill floor with high impact. Tanks are never truly bad.
- **Paige** tier=A dir=stable: Good, slightly better than Ivy, but 'support slop' — supports physically can't match carry agency right now.
- **Paradox** tier=C dir=falling: Not great despite his dislike for the hero; high-rank Paradoxes are building 'fake' bomb damage-farm builds, a sign the hero is cooked.
- **Pocket** tier=B dir=falling: Bottom of strong / top of decent. Would be broken without Counter Spell, and is a 'gen one' spirit burst hero power-crept by newer spirit burst DLC kits with better stats and kits.
- **Rem** tier=C dir=falling: Giga larp: most troll/smurf-hostile hero, so wait its win rate is skewed. When Rem is your top-gold player the team usually loses — it soaks map gold and lowers team win rate.
- **Seven** tier=S dir=rising: Most slept-on broken hero: 55.7% WR at highest elo, 55.4% all ranks. Best-in-game gun DPS plus CC and range, scales extremely well, and best low-elo solo-carry with Ricochet. Not the same as Victor because he has a gun and CC.
- **Shiv** tier=A dir=stable: Good, middle of the pack but not top 10; should get no more buffs. His movement is OP and his kit is cancerous — wants a minor rework.
- **Silver** tier=A dir=rising: Another DLC hero: very good, very tanky, does a lot of damage. Simple and OP.
- **Sinclair** tier=None dir=None: Possible S but not rankable until draft exists: efficient only with a favorable draft, cannot late-game carry, too skill/variable dependent. He refuses to value him as a real hero until draft.
- **Venator** tier=S dir=rising: Consistently strong and near the top with no single glaringly OP ability; easiest hero in the game by far — a first-time player dropped 30 kills and laughed.
- **Victor** tier=D dir=falling: Terrible/unpickable: over-nerfed because low elo complains. 'Late-game spirit scaler' is an oxymoron since spirit doesn't scale; he has no CC, bad HP per boon, no gun damage, and is the most counterable hero if you have movement and itemize.
- **Vindicta** tier=A dir=stable: Top of decent: good but not OP, has many counters and doesn't scale as hard as other carries.
- **Viper** tier=A dir=rising: Hot take: really good, under-loved, high win rate; damage output is crazy.
- **Viscous** tier=C dir=falling: Only good because Cube is OP; every other spell is weak and the splatter build dies to flying heroes. No individual scaling or late-game carry — you would never pick him to climb.
- **Warden** tier=A dir=rising: Actually really good — rated above his expected slot.
- **Wraith** tier=A dir=stable: Very good; 'annoying but you can still win' — the benchmark for the A-tier the game should aspire to.
- **Yamato** tier=A dir=stable: Strong ('good as fuck') but he hates the rework: a point-click, across-the-map 2 that now procs Stalker and melee effects with too much damage. Every Yamato opens 2-2 and point-clicks on ~18s cooldown; wants the 2 reworked.

## Reasoning patterns
- Isolates a single point of power to explain a whole hero: Haze is fine except one max-rank ult node granting one extra ult target, and that one change flipped her ult build from terrible to giga broken.
- Reads item against item slot economy: a 30s-CD ult lining up exactly with Divine Kevlar's 30s CD is what makes Mirage's uptime permanent — the nerf target is the item/cooldown alignment, not the numbers.
- Treats base regen as the most broken base stat via unit math (1 regen = 60 HP/minute, so win trades by that margin), concluding Holliday loses every lane trade and needs 2 regen.
- Distinguishes archetype-not-hero problems: 'late-game spirit scaler' is an oxymoron because spirit doesn't scale, so Victor has no role — and contrasts Seven because Seven has a gun and CC.
- Frames win-rate data against pick context: Rem's 52%-ish number is discounted because it is the most troll/smurfed hero, and he wants a 'top-gold Rem' filtered win rate to prove it lowers the team's odds.
- Attributes nerfs to developer reaction to low-elo complaints rather than strength — Victor is 'over-nerfed' and made unpickable by noob whining.
