# Vegas Deadlock Hero Tier List (26 June 2026) — Vegas (20260626)

## Meta thesis
Vegas reads the meta as tank-dominated and agency-inflated: the bottom half of the roster is balanced, the top half is overtuned, and his prescription is that the power level should go down, not up. Fights are gated by 5k-HP tanks that late spirit burst can no longer kill, so percent-max-HP healing (Rem), non-dispellable anti-heal/true damage (Venator) and mobile tank spirit-shred (Shiv) define picks, while support enablers (Rem, Viscous, Paige) multiply the best carries. Laning matters a little more than before, so strong laners/enablers rise, and the fastest pub farmers (Seven, Victor) win by converting creep speed into early walkers and a snowball.

## Reasoning patterns
- Reinterprets aggregate win rate as noisy data: Pocket looks like a 45.9% hero in E4+, but because 250+ games played adds ~8% win rate, he reads the low number as low-mastery dilution rather than weakness — the hero is broken for mains.
- Blames a system change, not a stat, for a hero's fall: Graves was 'inflated by the old Urn', so removing the objective plus repeated nerfs explains the collapse better than any single number.
- Derives picks from late-game damage math: because 5k-HP tanks survive late spirit burst, percent-max-HP healing (Rem) and non-dispellable anti-heal/true damage (Venator) become the defining value.
- Tests counterplay by walking the interaction: Indomitable is meant to answer Haze, but you just sleep-dagger the Indom and then Curse — so the 'counter' is fake.
- Compares two similar carries on mechanic details: Venator over Infernus because Venator's damage isn't dispellable and his HP cap (~5.5k) nearly doubles Infernus' (~3.5k), and he pivots to a grenade build that ignores Metal Skin.
