# Vegas Deadlock Hero Tier List (26 May 2026) — Vegas (20260526)

## Meta thesis
The game is decided at minute 15-20, and the urn (earn) fight determines hero strength more than anything else — this is the biggest 6v6 urn team-fight meta of all time. Because winning hard early hands the enemy a comeback urn (50% armor in a 60-meter radius), early-game comps that stomp lane actually lose; competitive teams that won early lost almost every game. So the tier list is effectively an urn-fight tier list: AoE, CC, and front-line HP value dominate, single-target and pure-lane heroes fall.

## Hero claims
- **Abrams** tier=B dir=rising: Good but mediocre on the urn with better frontliners available; would move to A if the urn gets nerfed.
- **Apollo** tier=C dir=falling: One of the best pre-10-minute heroes, but that no longer matters because stomping early gives the enemy the comeback urn. Only real value is Reposte cancelling Seven/McGinnis/Lash.
- **Bebop** tier=C dir=falling: Below average this patch; beam build is too easily counterable versus other ults, and he floats near 45% win rate.
- **Billy** tier=S dir=rising: Best frontliner this patch. Buffs plus the global health reduction and buffed Spirit Snatch let one slam gain ~900 HP on the urn, stacking to 5-6k HP with 40 armor — an elite Heroic Aura holder.
- **Calico** tier=D dir=falling: Garbage: lowest utility of any hero in mid-boss/urn fights, no CC, doesn't kill through the health pool. Would likely be decent if the urn meta were changed.
- **Celeste** tier=S dir=stable: Bottom of S/A. One of the best urn runners and urn fighters with AoE damage/amp, a bouncing ball, four abilities, mobility, and giga-late scaling.
- **The Doorman** tier=A dir=falling: Still very, very good and worth playing, but no longer Z-tier / top-five this patch.
- **Drifter** tier=C dir=stable: Not great but better than Calico, with real individual-duel strength; melee changes plus the Spirit Snatch buff make his damage solid.
- **Dynamo** tier=None dir=falling: Framed as OP but is actually bait: Black Hole gets countered by McGinnis ult one-framing him, Lash ults him off, Dispel/Sinclair/Vyper outvalue him. Not picked once across competitive regions.
- **Grey Talon** tier=B dir=stable: Simply a good hero this patch, per the creator.
- **Graves** tier=A dir=falling: Low-elo players call it S but in competitive it's roughly unpicked; worst early game in the game, and McGinnis does the same job far better (60% vs 52% win rate at E6). Bottom of A when balancing comp and pubs.
- **Haze** tier=D dir=falling: Garbage this patch — brings nothing valuable to urn fights; was OP one week ago, now the build gets swatted instantly.
- **Holliday** tier=B dir=rising: Actually decent, not bad at all, carried by the Magic Carpet buff.
- **Infernus** tier=C dir=stable: Top of C — fine and playable but countered by every top-seven hero. A floor-is-lava hero that loses value as ELO rises (less time on the ground) and gets cleansed by the multiple Dispel Magics per team in high ELO.
- **Ivy** tier=B dir=stable: Solid, one of the better decent supports for this meta — a good circle hero in a circle meta.
- **Kelvin** tier=B dir=falling: Really good right now because you can dome the urn, but the creator calls that a bug likely hotfixed within 48 hours; his tier drops if fixed.
- **Lady Geist** tier=D dir=falling: Giga fell off: not a snipe-lane meta and the urn is bad for her, though she snaps back up the moment the urn bug is fixed.
- **Lash** tier=S dir=rising: Broken always, averaging A+ every patch and inflated — a bad one-trick still wins over 50%. Good early/mid/late, flies, high HP, strong teamfight and 1v1; Spirit build benefited from the slam and Mystic Shot buffs.
- **McGinnis** tier=S dir=rising: Actually broken: maxed ult at the first urn fight one-frames a full-HP Dynamo even through Unstoppable, takes no skill (controller top damage), and has no counterplay. The turrets aren't the problem — the ult is.
- **Mina** tier=B dir=stable: Pretty mid, possibly underrated/balanced, but not enough games seen to be convinced.
- **Mirage** tier=None dir=falling: The best hero in the game who lacks an OP urn fight, so the urn meta drags his placement down; if the urn mattered less he'd be giga-broken. Single-target in an AoE meta, yet went 2-13 in one comp run and still got picked 15 times.
- **Mo & Krill** tier=B dir=stable: Not bad but hard to place; would climb if the urn became a king-of-the-hill that can't complete with enemies inside, letting him stack his one plus Scorn/Burrow.
- **Paige** tier=D dir=falling: Valued very low and completely unplayed in tourney and high ELO; a victim hero that just explodes, strictly worse than Rem who has an escape/heal.
- **Paradox** tier=S dir=rising: Z-tier turbo broken. Alchemical Fire + Echo Shard bomb does ~70k damage in grand finals, swaps people off the urn, dominates mid boss, stalls, and wins early/mid/late; the Mystic Shot buff is massive for her.
- **Pocket** tier=S dir=stable: Broken: banned 38 of ~40 tourney games, and in the two he wasn't banned he was the best character in the lobby. Affliction is the core problem.
- **Rem** tier=A dir=rising: Really good value, arguably as strong as Doorman. Strong teamfight healing with the bouncing-burst buff, great ult for fights, three is good for pre-fight wave clear.
- **Seven** tier=B dir=stable: Should be and is good, but wants to see more; S+ in Phantom-and-below, less fun/unclear in Eternus. Apollo's Reposte can cancel him.
- **Shiv** tier=A dir=rising: Still pretty broken with so much damage; the new Arctic Blast changes plus double dashes can randomly spawn ~8k damage on the urn.
- **Silver** tier=S dir=stable: Still way too good — the second-best tank — with huge early and whole-game value, the strongest hero at minutes 5/10/15. A DPS tank that dives backlines and wins duels; he isn't AoE but is the non-AoE hero you still want.
- **Sinclair** tier=A dir=stable: The ultimate potential man — great urn value and parry/bolt utility, but too inconsistent to be S without a draft (you can coin-flip into ~80% unfavorable matchups). Mid-A is a high placement.
- **Venator** tier=A dir=rising: Phenomenal — a huge fan of the grenade build. Ult max, grenades on mid boss, and grenades on a stacked urn give built-in anti-heal; one of the better M1 carries, ranked around/above Mirage.
- **Victor** tier=A dir=stable: Really good even after a nerf ('pretty LAR'). A gun hero with good early tempo; his death on the urn is a giga equalizer because he stuns for ages and amps allied AoE. Sleeper S-tier.
- **Vindicta** tier=D dir=falling: Garbage — a victim hero; playing her into Lash/Paradox every game is miserable. Slightly more urn utility than some but still junk.
- **Viscous** tier=D dir=falling: Garbage and got worse. Mid-lane is the worst terrain for him, ball/splatter do nothing, and the punch buff is effectively a nerf because Rebuttal has the highest buy rate of any patch, making him damage himself.
- **Vyper** tier=S dir=rising: Hella good — possibly S. Strong early and his ult is really valuable on the urn, held back only by the variance of being random-drafted into Lash/Billy.
- **Warden** tier=D dir=falling: The big loser — called F-tier / possibly the worst hero in the game. The gun-damage direction (Yoshi) is bad, no AoE nuking, was already bad last patch.
- **Wraith** tier=A dir=rising: Chill and pretty good — the best carry at deleting AoE slop. Ricochet Wraith makes Graves unplayable and does giga late AoE damage. Probably high placement.
- **Yamato** tier=S dir=stable: Good regardless of doubters. Her 2 gives 40 free spirit plus spirit-per-boon, letting her skip purple items, yet her one hits 700 (1,300 with Tankbuster+Reverb) while she's invulnerable for urn fights; scaling went from ~1.8-1.9 to 2.1-2.6.

## Reasoning patterns
- Frames a global/system change as the real hero buff: the global health reduction plus Billy's Spirit Snatch buff is what makes one slam worth ~900 HP and Billy the best frontliner.
- Reads the comeback urn (50% armor, 60m radius) as inverting the value of early-game comps — winning lane hard is a losing position, so a strong pre-10 hero like Apollo lands in C despite his numbers.
- Tracks an item's buy rate as a balance lever: Rebuttal's record pick rate turns Viscous's punch buff into a self-damage nerf, i.e. a buff that is really a nerf.
- Names a bug as temporary: Kelvin's ability to dome the urn is called a hotfix-in-48-hours bug and his placement is conditional on it being fixed, same for Lady Geist snapping back up.
- Distinguishes pilot/ELO from kit strength: Seven and Graves swing wildly between low ELO (S+/OP) and Eternus (unpicked), so the tier is a comp read, not a raw power read.
- Inferns intent from an omission: the biggest surprise is that Mirage received no nerf despite being giga-broken, showing the patch targeted the urn meta rather than raw single-target power.
