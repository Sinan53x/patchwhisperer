# Vegas Deadlock Hero Tier List (#2 April 2026) — Vegas (20260428)

## Meta thesis
Vegas reads the patch as an HP-inflation, gun-damage meta: everyone has too much HP and spirit armor, so spirit nukers can no longer one-combo and almost every 'bad spirit hero' is better played as gun. Fights are won by mobile bruisers with no tradeoff (Shiv, Silver, Mina, Vyper, Infernus) that fly around and one-shot, while immobile or burst-reliant heroes (Graves, Calico, Paradox, Grey Talon) just die. Lane matters less; map pressure, invades, mid-boss secure and carry-support (Ivy, Kelvin) win games, and the gun/spirit/green balance is broken by the damage-penalty stat.

- **Bebop** tier=A dir=stable: Gun Bebop very strong with lots of CC/stuns/lockdown; explicitly says don't build spirit.

- **Calico** tier=D dir=falling: No stuns, no map pressure, no kill potential; can't one-combo anymore because everyone gained HP, spirit armor and mobility. Worst spirit assassin.

## Reasoning patterns
- Reads blanket HP/spirit-armor inflation as the hidden driver behind many hero verdicts: because everyone gained HP and armor, one-combo spirit assassins (Calico, Pocket) lost their identity while sustained gun builds got better.
- Frames the meta as a role-relative ranking: Lady Geist and Warden are 'actually broken' in a vacuum but read as bad only because better heroes fill the exact same slot, so their tier is a function of rivals, not their own kit.
- Infers intent from item nerfs: the toxic bullets and echo shard nerfs plus the loss of kill gold are what actually deleted Graves and Vindicta, not any single kit change.
- Uses 'who would you rather pick' comparisons to define role dominance: you never pick Calico over Amira or Apollo, never Geist over Venator, which turns a mild kit into a D-tier verdict.
- Treats mobility as a stat-triangle violation: a hero that is tanky, high-damage AND mobile (Shiv, Silver) has no downside, so any added escape tool (Shiv ult, shotgun) is a systemic problem rather than a numbers tweak.
