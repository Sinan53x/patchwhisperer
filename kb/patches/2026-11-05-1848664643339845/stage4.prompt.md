# Reader corrections

Human-authored facts and judgments that override model output. Injected verbatim into

## 2026-09-19

- Infernus: not a pure gun hero. Roughly 50/50 between a gun build (burn synergy) and
  "Dashfernus", a full-spirit dash/mobility build. Both are mainstream, not niche.
- Vindicta: S tier is doubtful. Flyers got hurt this patch: Silver's Weighted Bola now
  grounds/interrupts flyers, and slows now affect air drag. Cross-hero effects like this
  (a buffed anti-air tool on a strong hero) must count against flyers.
- Calico: likely benefits from the tempo meta; re-evaluate upward.
- Haze: rated too high. The Fixation buff is good but T4 gun items are still weak, so
  gun carries that depend on them do not scale as the tier suggests. Compare against
  Venator, who is probably the better pick in that slot.
- General: reason about builds (which items, gun vs spirit, and how popular each build
  actually is) and about how heroes affect each other given who is strong right now,
  not just about a hero's own numbers.

## 2026-09-23

- Haze: not inherently a side-lane farming gun carry. That is how she is played in the
  current patch, but historically she often plays as a roamer/ganker (Smoke Bomb picks,
  Fixation on isolated targets). Her playstyle swings with balance and meta, so frame the
  farm-carry role as meta-dependent, not as her identity.
- Counter items: note the main counter items per archetype where relevant (e.g. Plated
  Armor vs gun carries, Phantom Strike vs flyers/mobile heroes), but remember counters
  have counters (e.g. Armor Piercing Rounds vs Plated Armor). Mention the key ones; do
  not try to list every counter item for every hero.

## 2026-11-03

- The Broker is gone from the game. It was a limited-time feature of City Never Sleeps.
  Never mention it, its 30-minute spawn or its shop as a current mechanic. Corrupted
  items came from the Broker and no longer exist, so never suggest them.
- Apollo (10-30): Riposte's Pommel Hit went from a stun to an immobilize and parry
  disable. That is a rework and a real loss, since it can no longer interrupt channels.
- General: crowd control changing type is never a plain buff, even when its duration rises.

## 2026-11-04

- Steam Vents still grant invisibility. Halloween only added +150% stamina regen on top.
  Never say they lost invisibility.
- General: an added effect is not a replacement. Say something was removed only when the
  notes say so.
- Lines under a "Street Brawl" header apply only to that mode. Never present them as
  main-game changes.
- Tiers come from measured match data, not from the notes. A heavily nerfed hero can still
  be the strongest hero: Baba took big nerfs on 10-30 and still has the top win rate.

## Previous meta thesis (knowledge base, pre-patch)

# Meta state
Last patch: Halloween 2026 + Gameplay Update (2026-10-30)

## Thesis
Games are won by converting a lead into Guardians/Walkers before the comeback economy catches up. Off-lane Haunt farm is still the income engine, but it now pays the trailing team too: Haunt bounty and growth are up, non-hero comeback gold is capped at 30%, tick souls feed the two poorest, and a team that is behind secures contested Haunt souls up to 50% faster. So the winners are one-cycle camp clearers who can also spend the lead fast, plus late scalers who now have a farm floor; teams that sit on an advantage bleed it into a jungle that keeps paying the loser.

## Economy and systems
- Comeback: non-hero comeback gold capped at 30% (was 26%), tick souls routed to the two lowest-econ players, deficit-scaled secure rate up to 50% faster on contested Haunt souls. Sitting on a lead is now a losing line.
- Kill income stays contested: the Soul Bag must be last-hit after the kill.
- Bounties: Guardian +10%, Walker +5%, objective share 30->25% (09-16, holds).
- Objective parries no longer reset the parry cooldown — Guardian/Shrine dives are one-shot.
- Respawn 38s at 20m. Global slows -20%; air drag ratio 0.5 (was 0.35) — airborne exits are slower, ground kiting stronger, flyers taxed harder.
- Steam Vents keep their invisibility and now also add +150% stamina regen.
- Cast-on-press holds; Healing Snacks 10% max HP unchanged.

## Map
Lanes renamed; four districts plus off-lane corridors. All neutrals are one of nine Haunt types that group and chase — bounty and growth +10%, HP and damage +10%. Tough Crates (58 souls, Heavy Melee only), Barrel Mimics, Sinner's Sacrifice variants, and Buff Containers rolling permanent Move Speed, Bullet/Spirit Resist and ability range; a Halloween candy-cauldron layer rewards Heavy Melee. Hubs: Sunken Plaza (~5-8 min, stamina strip) and Bell Tower (8+, ~280 souls, one rope, three vaults, map-wide bell). Green lane holds a roof/tree above the bridge. Arch Mother's side owns Sunken Plaza plus the theater.

## Archetype standing
- AoE one-cycle camp clearers: up — richer Haunts barely cost them HP (Seven, Infernus, McGinnis, Mina, Yamato).
- In-fight lifesteal bruisers: up — lifesteal cluster buffed while out-of-combat regen was cut (Victor, Infernus, Drifter).
- Heavy-melee frontline/tanks: stable — Torment Pulse +4% melee resist, but Hunter's Aura is LOS-gated and Decay/Healbane tax self-heal (Abrams, Mo & Krill, Billy, Shiv).
- Out-of-combat regen sustain: down — Radiant Regeneration is combat-only, Healing Booster delinked and trimmed (Baba, Shiv, Lady Geist, Celeste).
- Dedicated healers: down — Healbane now stacks across sources (Kelvin, Ivy, Rem).
- Max-range poke/gun carries: down — HVR velocity 60->45%, Express Shot -5%, worse air drag (Vindicta, Grey Talon, Paige).
- Roamers/pick assassins: mixed — slower tunnels and Shadow Weave needs 6s setup, but stamina vents feed re-engages (Lash, Holliday, Paradox, Calico).
- Lane bullies: slightly down — the comeback floor blunts snowball conversion (Paige, Apollo).
- Late scalers: up — non-hero comeback gold and tick souls fund the slow curve (Pocket, Mirage, Warden).
- Flyers: down — air drag 0.5 plus reliable grounding (Grey Talon, Celeste, Vindicta, Deadman Danny).

## Watchlist
- Average game length: if flat, comeback gold is going into fights, not items.
- Steal rate on contested Haunt souls: does the jungle become a fight zone?
- Radiant Regeneration buy rate and Juggernaut/lifesteal uptake — proxy for whether regen died or re-slotted into in-fight sustain.
- Double anti-heal comps (Healbane + Toxic Bullets/Crippling Headshot) into Kelvin/Ivy/Rem.
- Graves snapshot 56.5% WR / 40.9% PR still conflicts with the KB's 48.5% / 21% — recheck.
- Rat King win rate against pick rate.

## Recent history
- Halloween 2026 + Gameplay Update (2026-10-30): comeback levers (30% non-hero cap, tick souls to the two poorest, faster secure when behind), Haunt bounty/growth and HP/damage +10%, Steam Vents add +150% stamina regen, ~30 item moves — regen out, in-fight lifesteal and stacked anti-heal in.
- Shh... This Won't Hurt a Bit (2026-10-20): off-lane corridors and hub fights priced in; Haunt behaviors, Barrel Mimics, Steam Vents, Healing Snacks, instant casts; Graves gun destroys Venator traps.
- Paint the Town (2026-10-16): nine Haunt camp types replace all neutrals, contested Soul Bags, objective-parry cooldown reset removed, permanent-stat Buff Containers, six vote-gated new heroes.
- Minor Update - 10-14-2026: Baba frontline cut; Deadman Danny buff package; Radiant Regeneration third trim.
- Minor Update - 10-05-2026: trooper-speed regression fixed; Rat King and Sinclair trimmed.

## What to do

Write the verdict a strong player wants in the first 30 seconds.

1. **Patch size.** Classify: `major` (systems rework or many structural changes; content updates: map rework, new objectives, new heroes; the way games are played changes), `significant` (meaningful systems or economy moves plus broad tuning; the tier list will move), `minor` (tuning; a few heroes move a tier, no systems shift), `hotfix` (a handful of fixes/tweaks). Justify in one or two sentences naming the drivers. Change counts alone do not decide size; a 5-line economy change can be major.
2. **Headline.** One sentence, the thing to remember.
3. **Meta thesis.** State the previous thesis in one sentence, the new thesis in one or two, and whether this is a continuation, a shift, or a reversal. The thesis is about *how the game is won* (tempo vs scaling, which archetypes carry, what the fights look like), not a list of heroes.
4. **Winners and losers.** 3-5 each, ranked, with the driver. Include indirect movers where justified; mark them as such.
5. **Non-obvious calls.** 2-4 claims a casual reader of the notes would miss: unlisted heroes that moved, a "general" change that is really a targeted nerf, a build that died, a sleeper item. Each with a confidence.
6. **Uncertainties and what to watch.** What you are unsure about and what data would settle it in the first week.

7. **Biggest changes.** The 4-7 things a player must know before their next game, ranked by how much they change how you play. This is the first thing on the page, read in ten seconds by someone who will not read anything else.
   - The test: would a player do something different in their next game because of this? A hero who stops being playable, an item a build depended on, an objective that now works differently, a new way to win.
   - Mix heroes, items and systems as the patch warrants, including objectives such as the Mid-Boss. Do not fill a quota.
   - Leave out plain number trims. A 10 second longer ult cooldown or a 4% stronger pickup never belongs here, however many of them there are.
   - `subject` is the exact hero, item or system name as written in the analyses above.
   - `title` is at most 10 plain words a new player understands. `why` is one short sentence with the decisive number from the notes. Report numbers only as the notes state them.
   - `effect` is `up` if this is good for the subject or for players who use it, `down` if bad, `neutral` for a change in how it works.
